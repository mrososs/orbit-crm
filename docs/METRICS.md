# Metric definitions & dataset assumptions

All figures are computed from the persisted records at render time by the pure functions in
`src/app/core/analytics/metrics.ts`. Nothing is hard-coded, and no trend is shown unless it can be
computed from real records.

## Periods and comparisons

- **Presets:** last 30 days, last 90 days, last 6 calendar months (including the current month),
  last 12 calendar months, year to date, or a custom inclusive range.
- **Previous period:** the period of identical length immediately before the selected one.
- **Comparability:** comparisons (percentage changes, point changes, "previous" values and the
  dashed previous-period series) are shown **only when the whole previous period lies inside
  recorded history** (on or after the earliest deal's creation date). Otherwise the UI states that
  no prior data exists instead of showing a misleading change.
- **Chart buckets:** weekly (ISO weeks) for ranges up to 92 days, calendar months otherwise. The
  previous-period line is aligned bucket-by-bucket by shifting each prior close date forward by the
  period length.

## KPIs

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Total revenue | Sum of `value` for deals in **Won** whose `closedAt` falls in the period. |
| Pipeline value | Sum of `value` for open deals (New, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation) — a snapshot as of now, independent of the period. Won and Lost deals are excluded. |
| Weighted pipeline | Pipeline value × stage probability (New 10 %, Qualified 25 %, Proposal 50 %, Negotiation 75 %). |
| Active deals | Number of open deals now. The hint and trend refer to deals **created** in the period vs. the previous period. |
| Win rate | Won ÷ (Won + Lost) for deals **closed** in the period. Change is shown in percentage points. |
| Average deal size | Revenue ÷ number of deals won in the period. |
| Open follow-ups | Activities not yet completed. *Overdue* = due before today (calendar day); *due today* = due today. |
| Avg. sales cycle | Mean days from `createdAt` to `closedAt` for deals won in the period. |

## Charts

| Chart | Data |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue performance / trend | Closed-won revenue per bucket (+ aligned previous period when comparable). |
| Sales pipeline / pipeline analysis | Open deals per stage: count, total and weighted value. |
| Lead sources | Contacts **created** in the period per acquisition source. |
| Deals won per month | Won deals per calendar month by `closedAt` (tooltip adds revenue). |
| Team performance | Per member: revenue, wins/losses, win rate, open pipeline, average deal; attainment = revenue ÷ (quarterly quota × days in period ÷ 91.25). |
| Deal conversion | Cohort of deals **created** in the period; a deal "reached" every stage up to its current one, Won deals reached all, Lost deals reached the stage recorded in `lostAtStage`. |
| Deal size distribution | Deals created in the period by value band (< 10k, 10–25k, 25–50k, 50–100k, ≥ 100k) and outcome. |
| Lead source insights | Per source: leads created, deals created, revenue and win rate (deals use their originating source). |

The dashboard team filter scopes deals, activities and contacts by owner; reports add a lead-source
filter.

## Company rollups

- **Open pipeline** — open deals linked to the company.
- **Won revenue** — Won deals linked to the company (all time).
- **Total deal value** — won revenue + open pipeline (Lost deals excluded).

## Seeded dataset

Generated by `src/app/core/data/seed/generate-seed.ts` with a fixed PRNG seed (`20260603`,
mulberry32). The same anchor date always yields the same dataset.

- **Anchor:** dates are generated relative to the day the workspace is first seeded in a browser,
  so the demo always looks current. The anchor is stored (`seededAt`) and persists until reset.
- **Volume:** 8 team members, 30 fictional companies, 100 contacts, 80 deals (28 won, 20 lost,
  32 open), 120 activities (80 completed, 40 open — including 8 overdue and 5 due today) and 363
  timeline events derived from the records.
- **History:** closed deals span the last 12 months with a growth trend (more wins in recent
  months); open deals were created within the last ~100 days; companies were added 15–18 months
  ago; contact creation is skewed toward recent months.
- **Currency:** all amounts are USD (`WORKSPACE_CURRENCY`). Changing language changes formatting
  only — values are never converted.
- **Realism rules:** deal values scale with company size; a contact always exists before its
  deals; won/lost deals carry `closedAt`, lost deals carry `lostAtStage`; quotas are calibrated to
  give attainment between roughly 60 % and 150 %; about one in five seeded touchpoints is completed
  1–3 days late.
- **Fictional data:** company names, people, domains and phone numbers are invented; where a
  numbering plan reserves fictional ranges (e.g. US 555-01xx, UK 020 7946 0xxx, FR 01 99 00) they
  are used.
